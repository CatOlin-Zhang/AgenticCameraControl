> The devices listed below are **S-class** cameras — they can use the **full** skill capability set (see the capability matrix in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)). Devices not listed here only support **partial** functionality: **O-class** (third-party ONVIF-only) and **J-class** (JCP).
